Window management bench

Benchmark in progress.

Seven independent challenges, in any order. Each Open button opens native desktop windows (they may appear behind the browser). The work is done to the windows themselves — move, resize, maximize, minimize, snap, close, and find them.

1. Move a window

Open the pair, then drag the Gauge window by its title bar until it sits entirely inside the dashed frame drawn in the Dock frame window.

2. Resize a window

Resize the Sizer window by dragging its edges or corner until its size is inside the target it shows (it displays its own size as you go). Do not maximize it.

3. Maximize, read, restore

The Ledger window shows its Total column only when it is (nearly) full-screen. Maximize it, enter the total here, then restore the Ledger to its normal size.

4. Minimize and restore

An always-on-top Chat window covers the Approve button in the Inbox window. Minimize Chat (closing it ends the call), press Approve in the Inbox, then restore the Chat window.

5. Snap to half the screen

Snap the Notes window to the half of the screen (Windows key + arrow, or drag its title bar against that edge of the screen).

6. Close only the right windows

This opens three Tip of the day windows and a Work log window that look alike. Close every Tip of the day window and keep the Work log open.

7. Find a buried window

A small Beacon window opens completely hidden behind a large Maintenance overlay. Bring the Beacon to the front (taskbar, Alt+Tab, or move the overlay aside) and press its Acknowledge button. Do not close the overlay.
