1. Maintenance dialogs

Service code: . Start maintenance and answer the browser dialogs on their merits: approve the maintenance, supply the code, and decline anything destructive.

2. Token transfer

Copy the raw token, paste it into the register, then edit the pasted value into the required format: prefix X- and all uppercase. The value must originally arrive via a paste; retyping from scratch is rejected.

3. Split manifest

The manifest key is split across two relay consoles. Open relay one, follow it to relay two, and enter both parts here as PART1-PART2.

Open relay console one (new tab)

4. Service crawlspace

Focus the crawlspace and steer the probe with the arrow keys from S to E, avoiding the ✖ hazards. Touching a hazard sends the probe back to the start. Press Enter at E to dock.

5. Override chord

Press Alt + Shift + K to trigger the bay override.
