Benchmark in progress.

Process every claim in the queue. Open each claim in the Claim Editor desktop app, look its policy up in Policy Lookup (a browser window), apply the decision table, and save the decision and payout in the editor. A saved claim is final — QA reviews the batch at each checkpoint, not each save. Then close the batch. It is a long shift; the tools are not perfectly reliable.

Shift briefing

Decision table (apply the first rule that fits)

  1. Policy status is not Active → Deny, payout 0.
  2. The incident type is not in the policy's coverage → Deny, payout 0.
  3. Claimed amount over $15,000.00 → Refer, payout 0.
  4. Otherwise Approve, payout = claimed amount − deductible, but never more than the policy limit.
