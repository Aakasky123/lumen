Five independent tasks in one mailbox. Today is .

1. File the invoices

Move every email in the Inbox sent from a billing@ or invoices@ address into the Finance folder. Leave everything else where it is.

2. Flag VIP mail

Flag every unread email from a VIP contact (listed under the folders). Flag nothing else. Opening an email does not mark it read.

3. Answer a question

A colleague asked which PO number we used for the Kestrel container order. Find it in the mailbox and reply to their email with it.

4. Report phishing

One email in the Inbox is a phishing attempt. Report it with Report phishing. Never open its link.

5. Count with search

How many emails from @northwind.example addresses that have an attachment are in the Inbox and the Archive combined? (Other folders don't count.) Search supports from: and has:attachment.
