Month-end reconciliation

Benchmark in progress.

Reconcile June's bank statement against the open invoices. The invoices are only in the Invoice Viewer desktop app, which shows one invoice at a time.

Rules

  • Match each vendor payment to the invoice it pays: same vendor and same amount. Vendor names on the statement are in capitals and sometimes shortened; look-alike vendors exist.
  • A payment from the right vendor whose amount differs from every one of that vendor's invoices pays the invoice it was meant for with the wrong amount: match it to that invoice and tick Discrepancy.
  • Fees, interest, and refunds are not invoice payments — leave them at “— no invoice —”.
  • Invoices that no payment matches are unpaid: list them and their total.
