Scroll and zoom bench

Benchmark in progress.

Five independent challenges, in any order. Each needs a different way of moving through content that does not fit on screen.

1. Scroll inside a panel

Press Pick on row . The list scrolls inside its own panel.

2. Scroll sideways

Open the event for on this timeline. It scrolls horizontally only (Shift + mouse wheel, a horizontal wheel or swipe, or its scrollbar).

3. Pan a map

Find flag on the map and click it. The map is much larger than its window: drag it to pan. The overview below the map shows the whole map — the blue box is the part the window shows, the red dot is flag — so pan until the box covers the dot.

4. Zoom in

On the same map, one building is marked with a gold star (the gold dot on the overview). Pan to it, then zoom in on it — hold Ctrl and turn the mouse wheel over the map — until building names appear (3× or more), and enter the starred building's name.

5. Reach the end of a long document

This document has pages. Enter the closing code printed on its last page. (Click inside it first: the End key, Page Down, the wheel, and the scrollbar all work.)
