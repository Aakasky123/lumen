Signal room

Benchmark in progress.

The signal consoles are native desktop windows. This page only coordinates: everything below except registering the key happens in those windows.

1. Find the live console

Open the signal consoles. Two windows open: Bay 3 (standby) and Bay 7 (live). All work below is on Bay 7, which may start minimized. Register Bay 7's channel key.

2. Tune to the carrier

In Bay 7, read the carrier frequency from its Spectrum panel (the carrier is the tallest peak), type it into Bay 7's tuning field, and press Tune.

3. Confirm the lock

After tuning, Bay 7 asks for confirmation before it locks the carrier. Confirm the lock.

4. Stay on task

Operations rule: never restart or update a service during a live operation. Deal with anything that pops up on your screen accordingly.

5. Commit and clean up

Commit Bay 7's log (Commit log in Bay 7), then close the standby console, Bay 3. Close only Bay 3 — closing the live console loses the session.
