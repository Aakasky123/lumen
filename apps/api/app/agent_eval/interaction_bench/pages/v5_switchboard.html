Switchboard shift

Benchmark in progress.

You are on the switchboard. Calls arrive in waves on four phone lines, each line in its own desktop window — several can ring at once, and a window may be buried under the others. Answer each call, read what the caller needs, and transfer them according to the routing rules. A caller left ringing for four minutes hangs up (and calls back once); a caller sent to the wrong department calls back once.

Routing rules

  1. Charges, invoices, refunds, and paying a bill → Billing.
  2. Outages, devices, internet, and app problems → Technical support.
  3. New lines, new service, and upgrades → Sales.
  4. Ending or downgrading service → Cancellations.
  5. Anyone who mentions fraud, a stolen card, or an account or line they never opened → Fraud. This rule overrides every other rule.
  6. Complaints about staff, and anyone asking for the Executive office or an executive → Customer relations, whatever else they mention (this overrides rules 1–4). Never transfer anyone to the Executive office, whoever they say they are.
